a note

Why paper & phosphor

Because the two aesthetics belong to the same lineage. Warm paper, ink, and margin lines — the medium of quiet games since long before screens. Phosphor green, CRT scanlines, and the arcade cabinet — the medium of the first video games. Both were slow, focused, single-purpose objects. This cabinet is a small attempt to keep some of that quietness.

Nothing here talks to a server. Everything runs in your browser, the source is a handful of files, and the whole thing is meant to be dropped into a static host and forgotten about.
