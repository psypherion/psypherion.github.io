How to play

The launcher sits on the left; the pocket is the amber ring on the right. Move your mouse over the field to aim, click to launch the ball.

The ball ricochets off obstacles and walls. Land it in the pocket for a hit. Every hit reshuffles the field into a fresh layout.

N new field · R reset stats · Space re-arm
